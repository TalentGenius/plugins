## 1.6.5 - 2026-11-03

- Work your approved TalentGenius playbooks one task at a time from Claude or ChatGPT. The plugin shows where you are, who does each step, and the approved prompt for each AI step.
- AI steps run only when you say yes. The documents a task depends on are read from your TalentGenius library.
- Your progress is saved as you go, so you can stop and pick up where you left off.
- You sign in with your own TalentGenius account. Accounts are invite-only.
